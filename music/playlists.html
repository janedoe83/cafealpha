We Shall Not Be Moved: A Chronology of US Protest Songs

US-American songs covering socialist, anarchist, feminist, anti-fascist, anti-war, anti-imperialist, anti-racist, abolitionist, pro-worker, and/or pro-LGBTQ themes. I am focusing on the US for several reasons, including the simple fact that the US has a huge music scene in English. Moreover, as the foremost imperial power, the US carries an outsized responsibility for the ongoing atrocities in the world, which means the people living in the US have a responsibility to hold their government accountable through protst and other means.
